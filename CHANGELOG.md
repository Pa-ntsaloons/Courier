# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Inbox gold total in the top-right of the Inbox. Hover it for a breakdown: auction sales, auction refunds, gold from players (by sender) and other mail, plus any COD you'd owe
- Recent no longer shows the character you're on, people on other rulesets or unconnected realms, or your own alts on the other faction

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Contacts button next to the "To:" box on the Send Mail tab
- Alts: your characters on the same faction and ruleset, with Syndicator integration
- Recent: the last 20 people you mailed, tagged Alt, Friend, Battle.net or Guild
- Friends, Battle.net friends (same game, faction and ruleset) and Guild lists
- Class icons and class colours throughout
- Support for WoW Forever's region-wide names with surnames, and its rulesets
- Slash commands: `/courier alts`, `forget`, `clearrecent`, `debug`
